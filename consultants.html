CONSULTANT REQUIREMENTS

Who Can Apply?

Consultant applicants should meet the professional requirements below before submitting an application.

Master's Degree

Must hold an MSc or equivalent master's degree in a recognized health discipline.

Medical Practitioner

Must be a qualified medical or health practitioner with appropriate professional training.

Health Institution Experience

Must have professional experience in a hospital, pharmacy, clinic, or other relevant health institution.

Minimum 5 Years Experience

Must have at least five years of professional experience in a health institution.

Practicing or Retired

Qualified professionals may apply whether they are currently practicing or retired.

Professional Communication

Applicants should be able to communicate professional health knowledge clearly and responsibly.
